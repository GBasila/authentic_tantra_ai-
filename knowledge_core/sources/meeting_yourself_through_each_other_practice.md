# Meeting Yourself Through Each Other

*A Practice for Couples · Знакомство с собой через отношения с партнёром*

---

## 1. Find your fighting pattern

- When we fight, what do I usually do — attack, go silent, withdraw, defend myself?
- What is happening in my body at that moment?
- What do I really want you to hear, but can't bring myself to say?
- What words, tones of voice, or gestures trigger my automatic reaction?
- What thoughts flash automatically in my head: *«he/she doesn't care»*, *«I'm alone»*?
- Where does this raw spot come from? Whose voice is hiding behind this reaction?
- Can you guess where your partner's raw spot lives — and how you, without meaning to, step on it?

---

## 2. Reach for each other · vulnerability

- What is the deepest fear that stirs in you when you think about our relationship?
- What do you need to hear from me to feel safe?
- What are you most afraid I will do if you actually ask for what you truly need?

---

## 3. Revisit a rupture moment

- Describe the painful episode as if you were a neutral observer. What did you do, what did your partner do?
- What deeper feelings do you recognise beneath your surface reaction? What deeper feelings do you sense beneath your partner's reaction?
- If we could replay that conversation today, what is the first sentence you would say to bring safety back?

---

## 4. Keep your love alive

- Recall a small warming moment from the last month that shifted the way you see me.
- Describe our shared dream 5–10 years from now. What is one step each of us can already take today toward it?
- What is one small daily action you can do so that I physically feel: you are here, you are choosing me?
- One phrase I will say when I feel us drifting apart.

---

## How to use

- **One person speaks at a time.** The other only mirrors: *«I heard you say…»*. No comments, no advice, no *«me too»*.
- **The more withdrawn partner begins.** In every couple, one tends to pursue and one tends to retreat. The retreating partner speaks first.
- **Sit close enough to whisper. Far enough to breathe.**
- **Take three slow breaths together before starting each block.**
- **You may pause at any point.** Not finishing a block is fine. Being seen fully is what matters, not covering the material.
